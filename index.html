How do I export my activities from Garmin?

Quick: activities CSV

  1. Open connect.garmin.com in a desktop browser and go to Activities → All Activities.
  2. Scroll to the bottom until every activity you want has loaded (the export only includes loaded rows).
  3. Click Export CSV at the top right and drop the file here.

Complete history: account data export

  1. In Garmin Connect, open Account settings → Data Management → Export Your Data and request the export. Garmin emails you a download link.
  2. Unzip it and open DI_CONNECT/DI-Connect-Fitness. Drop every *_summarizedActivities.json file here.

You can add several files; activities that are already loaded are skipped.
